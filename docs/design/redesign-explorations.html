Reading the five

    
01 Refit is the one to build if you like the app and only want the noise gone — it is
    the same tokens, the same markup, an afternoon of CSS. 02 Bench and 05 Studio are
    the two real forks in the road: Bench doubles down on being an instrument (figures, density,
    no decoration), Studio makes it a calm product (rounded, tinted, roomy). 03 Dossier is
    the one that best fits what the data actually is — legal memos judged criterion by criterion —
    and it is the biggest departure in voice while changing the least structure. 04 Console
    is the cheapest strong opinion, and the only one that makes monospace a decision rather than an
    accident; its weak point is 200-word judge paragraphs set in mono.

    
The pipeline handoff is separable from all of it. The pattern — rail shows the carried run,
    every stage ends in one action naming the next stage by number, and the run travels as a query
    param — can go into the app you have now, in any of these five skins.
